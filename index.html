Privacy Policy for Dabba Dash

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Dabba Dash ("the game") is a mobile game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

Summary

The game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. It does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress is saved only on your own phone: coins, best score, unlocked scooters, upgrades, mission and achievement progress, and your sound and vibration settings. This data never leaves your device through the game. If your phone's own backup service (for example Google's device backup) is switched on, it may include this data as part of your personal device backup, under Google's terms.

You can delete this data at any time by using "Reset progress" in the game's Settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

The game uses these Android permissions:

  • Vibration, for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Audio settings, to play music and sound effects.
  • Network access is declared by the app framework, but the game does not send or receive any data and works fully offline.

Children

The game does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising or tracking services. It is distributed through Google Play, whose own privacy policy applies to the store and to your Google account.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.
